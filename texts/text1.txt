How to Play

Make your predictions. Score points. Climb the leaderboard.

01 — Choose your matches

Pick from the upcoming matches available in Clash Eleven. Choose the games you want to predict before predictions close.

PICK YOUR MATCHES

02 — Make your prediction

First, choose who wins — home team, draw, or away team. Then predict the exact final score.

You can change your prediction anytime before it locks.

PREDICTIONS LOCK

10 MINUTES BEFORE KICK-OFF

03 — Score points

Your points depend on how close your prediction is to the final result:

EXACT SCORE
+5 POINTS
Predict the exact final score.

WINNER + GOAL DIFFERENCE
+3 POINTS
Predict the correct winner and the correct goal difference, but not the exact score.

CORRECT WINNER
+1 POINT
Predict the correct winner with a different score and goal difference.

MAXIMUM: +5 POINTS PER MATCH

04 — Climb the leaderboard

Your points add up across your matches. Compete with everyone on the global leaderboard or challenge your friends on private leaderboards.
