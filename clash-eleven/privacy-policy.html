Section 01 Telemetry & Identity

Information We Collect

In accordance with analytical minimization principles, we only capture structured data records strictly necessary to validate tactical predictions, verify match timelines, and deliver computational leaderboards.

Data Classification Telemetry Scope
Device Identifiers Cryptographic UUIDs, OS kernel versions, client display metrics, and coarse regional network addresses to prevent multi-account collision.
Account Credentials Salted authentication hashes, verified email contacts, unique player handle identifiers, and notification preferences.
Prediction History Granular timestamps of score submissions, confidence weight parameters, live match delta calculations, and tournament bracket selections.
Section 02 Processing Architecture

How We Use Information

Every data ingest is bound to deterministically auditable objectives within the forecasting cycle:

  • calculate

    Calculating Match Points

    Real-time compilation of match event deltas against user score vectors upon referee whistle confirmation.

  • leaderboard

    Leaderboard Synchronization

    Global and private community table re-ranking via physical monolithic sequence updates.

  • notifications

    Push Notifications

    Discrete fixture kickoff alerts, match lock notices (T-minus 15 minutes), and verified point settlements.

Section 03 Third Parties

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We uphold zero behavioral monetization. We do not sell, rent, or distribute player prediction data or device metrics to ad-tech syndicates or external broker networks.

Data is routed solely to audited technical infrastructure nodes: cloud compute providers enforcing at-rest AES-256 encryption, transactional messaging relays, and official football statistical feed providers for outcome verification.

Section 04 Lifecycle

Data Retention & Account Deletion

Match forecasts are held across active competition seasons to preserve permanent league records. Upon an explicit account deletion directive, player credentials and device associations are instantly scrubbed from active clusters within 72 hours, leaving only anonymized historic fixture percentages.
